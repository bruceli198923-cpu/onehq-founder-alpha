# OneHQ V1 - Founder Alpha

This Alpha is a usable product prototype for testing the OneHQ operating philosophy in practice.

## What It Includes

- Founder HQ with multiple independent companies.
- Each company has its own AI CEO, Brain, Workforce, Projects, Deliverables, Approvals and local memory.
- CEO Command as the primary control surface.
- Natural-language Founder goals that create projects automatically.
- Lightweight Living Office where staff state maps to real project/task state.
- Deliverables Center where completed work must produce a visible artifact.
- Company Brain with Knowledge, Principles, Decisions, Lessons and Feedback.
- Training flow for company-level memory and employee-level memory.
- Founder-controlled employee creation with role, department, reporting line, responsibilities, engine and authority.
- Governance screen with authority matrix and OneHQ Agent Protocol.
- Chairman Inbox behavior for Money and Legal approval.

## How To Test

1. Open `index.html`.
2. Choose the OneHQ company or create a new company.
3. Send a natural-language command to the Company AI CEO.
4. Click `Advance Work` to simulate execution, handoff, review and deliverable creation.
5. Visit Living Office to inspect staff state.
6. Open Deliverables to review produced work.
7. Add Founder feedback in Company Brain.
8. Approve or reject governance-sensitive items in Dashboard.

## Current Alpha Boundaries

- All state is saved locally in the browser.
- AI execution is simulated in-product rather than connected to live model routing.
- File attachments are recorded as context names, not uploaded to a server.
- Voice input is represented in the UX, but not yet connected.
- The purpose is product validation and operating-model iteration before backend implementation.
